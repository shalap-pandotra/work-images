Product Update

Introducing new ways to
engage with your community

Two new post types for admins and moderators: Ask Me Anything, for live Q&A sessions, and Announcements, for updates your members can't miss.

New Post Types
💬 What is an "Ask Me Anything"?
A live Q&A post that takes questions for a set duration, with each question getting its own thread.
📣 What is an Announcement?
A post that also appears as a pop-up when members open Community, so important updates don’t get missed.
  • Admin & Moderator Only: Only admins and moderators can create these posts, but every member can engage with them.

Moderators

Share the work of running your community

Assign moderators to the channels you choose, so conversations stay on track without you being everywhere at once.

  • Channel-Level Access: Moderators can see the whole club, but can only moderate the channels you assign them.
  • Built-in Moderation Tools: Moderators can create any type of post, delete posts, comments and messages, and pin chats.
  • Easy Setup: Add moderators from Team and roles in Community Settings.
